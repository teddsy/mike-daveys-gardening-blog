How the demo basket works

  1. Browse a category or a crop. Choose page view (cards) or list view (one line per product).
  2. Add lines to the basket. It is saved in this browser only.
  3. Open the basket to check the total. You can print a summary for your own notes. No payment is taken, and the order is not sent anywhere.
